## Hostinger free domain vs separate

Hostinger 12M Premium includes free `.com` yr1. Good deal if you buy Hostinger anyway — see [Hostinger Review](/blog/hostinger-review-nepal-blogging). Renewal after yr1 is ~$10–15/yr — **TODO verify NPR**.

Trade-off: Free domain locks you to Hostinger for yr1 (transfer after 60 days). If you want portability, buy on Namecheap and point A record to Hostinger IP + Cloudflare.

## .com.np free — for Nepalis

Free via Mercantile (register.mos.com.np) with citizenship scan. Takes 1–3 days, not instant. Good for personal/portfolio, but not for business that needs fast DNS. No eSewa needed — free. **TODO: Test timeline, screenshot form.**
