## Step 3 — Add Cloudflare (free, secondary)

1. Add site to Cloudflare → scan DNS → copy nameservers — **TODO screenshot**.
2. At domain registrar (Namecheap or Hostinger), paste Cloudflare nameservers.
3. In Cloudflare, set A record to Hostinger IP, proxied (orange cloud) ON. Wait 5 min – 2 h.
4. SSL mode: Full (if Hostinger SSL installed) — **TODO test**.

Why Cloudflare? Cuts TTFB ~40% from Nepal — see [Pillar](/blogging-hosting#cloudflare).

## Troubleshooting Nepal-specific

- **Payment fails:** Try another Visa/Mastercard, or use bank virtual card — eSewa wallet direct not always — **TODO verify**.
- **Slow after install:** Enable Cloudflare → Purge cache.
- **.com.np free:** Needs citizenship docs, 1–3 days — not instant.
